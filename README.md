# 🤖 AI Interview Agent

> An AI-powered interview preparation platform that generates personalized interview questions from your resume, conducts Technical & HR mock interviews, provides intelligent AI feedback, and offers a seamless credit-based experience.

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://ai-interview-agent-llpy.onrender.com/

---

## ✨ Features

- 📄 Upload Resume (PDF)
- 🤖 AI-generated interview questions based on your resume
- 💼 Practice Technical & HR interview rounds
- 📝 Get intelligent AI-powered feedback
- 💳 Credit-based interview access
- 💸 Buy credits securely using Razorpay
- 🔐 Google Authentication with Firebase
- 🎨 Smooth animations with Framer Motion
- 📱 Responsive UI
- ☁️ Full Stack deployment on Render

## 📖 How It Works

1. Sign in using Google.
2. Upload your resume (PDF).
3. AI analyzes your resume.
4. Generate personalized interview questions.
5. Choose Technical or HR interview.
6. Answer AI-generated questions.
7. Receive detailed AI feedback.
8. Use credits or purchase more through Razorpay.
